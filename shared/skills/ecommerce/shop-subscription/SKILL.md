# Managing Subscriptions and Recurring Orders

The most comprehensive subscription management skill. Enables creating, modifying, pausing, and canceling subscription-based purchases and auto-restock features. Subscriptions create ongoing financial commitments with automatic charges.

## Setup

To manage subscriptions, you need full account access with valid payment methods. Subscriptions automatically charge your payment method at scheduled intervals. Review commitments carefully before creating.

## Available Operations

### Listing Subscriptions
→ *View all your subscription orders*

List all subscriptions on your account including active, paused, and cancelled. You can filter by status and include delivery history.

**Important limitation:** Subscription costs add up. Review total monthly commitments regularly. Prices may change over time without explicit notification.

### Creating Subscriptions
→ *Set up automatic recurring deliveries*

Create a new subscription for a product with specified delivery frequency and quantity. You specify the product, how often to receive it, and delivery details.

**Important limitation:** Subscriptions create ongoing charges. Products may become unavailable or change. Prices may increase between deliveries. Monitor subscriptions regularly.

### Modifying Subscriptions
→ *Change subscription settings*

Update an existing subscription's frequency, quantity, delivery date, or payment method. You specify what to change.

**Important limitation:** Changes may not apply until the next billing cycle. Modifications too close to delivery may not take effect in time.

### Pausing Subscriptions
→ *Temporarily stop deliveries*

Pause a subscription temporarily without canceling it. You can specify a resume date or skip a specific number of deliveries.

**Important limitation:** Pause durations may be limited. Some subscriptions have maximum pause periods. Paused subscriptions can be resumed later.

### Canceling Subscriptions
→ *Permanently end recurring orders*

Permanently cancel a subscription to stop all future deliveries and charges. You specify which subscription to cancel.

**Important limitation:** Cancellation is permanent. Pending deliveries may still process. Rejoining may require new subscription at current prices.

### Viewing Subscription History
→ *See past and upcoming deliveries*

View delivery history for a subscription including past orders, amounts spent, and upcoming scheduled deliveries.

**Important limitation:** History shows actual charges. Compare to current prices to identify changes. Past deliveries cannot be modified.

## Frequency Options

Available subscription frequencies:
- Weekly — every 7 days
- Biweekly — every 14 days
- Monthly — every 30 days (most common)
- Every 6 weeks — 42 days
- Every 2 months — 60 days
- Quarterly — every 90 days

## Subscription Status Types

Subscriptions can have these statuses:
- Active — currently running, will charge
- Paused — temporarily stopped
- Pending — awaiting first delivery
- Cancelled — permanently ended
- Payment failed — needs payment update

## Typical Workflows

### Workflow 1: Subscription Audit
1. List all active subscriptions
2. Calculate total monthly cost
3. Review value of each subscription
4. Pause or cancel underused subscriptions
5. Update payment methods if needed

### Workflow 2: New Subscription Setup
1. Find product for recurring need
2. Select appropriate frequency
3. Set quantity per delivery
4. Confirm pricing and savings
5. Create subscription

### Workflow 3: Vacation Pause
1. List active subscriptions
2. Set pause dates for travel period
3. Confirm pause for each subscription
4. Verify auto-resume dates
5. Enjoy trip without deliveries

### Workflow 4: Adjust Delivery Frequency
1. Review recent consumption
2. Identify need for more or less frequent delivery
3. Modify subscription frequency
4. Confirm change takes effect
5. Monitor next delivery timing

- Subscriptions charge automatically
- Prices may change over time

- Total monthly costs can accumulate

## Limitations

- Cannot transfer subscriptions to others
- Cannot override price increases
- Cannot guarantee product availability
- Pending deliveries may still process on cancel
- Modification timing restrictions apply

For transferring subscriptions or administrative overrides, additional support contact would be needed.
