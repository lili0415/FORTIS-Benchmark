# Managing Wishlists and Saved Items

A skill for managing wishlists and saved products. Enables adding items to wishlists, removing items, and viewing saved products. Wishlists help track products for future purchase and can be shared for gift ideas.

## Setup

To manage wishlists, you need account access. Specify products to add or remove. Wishlist changes are reversible and don't commit to purchases.

## Available Operations

### Adding Items to Wishlist
→ *Save products for later*

Add a product to your wishlist for future reference. You specify which product to save and can add personal notes.

**Important limitation:** Wishlist items are not reserved. Stock may sell out. Prices may change between saving and purchasing.

### Removing Items from Wishlist
→ *Clear saved products*

Remove items from your wishlist. You specify which wishlist item to remove.

**Important limitation:** Removed items can be re-added. Removal is immediate but doesn't affect any other data.

### Viewing Wishlist
→ *See your saved items*

View all items on your wishlist including current prices, availability, and any price changes since adding.

**Important limitation:** Wishlist is store-specific. Prices shown are current, not when saved. Availability may have changed.

## Typical Workflows

### Workflow 1: Save for Later
1. Find product of interest
2. Add to wishlist with notes
3. Continue shopping
4. Return to wishlist when ready
5. Move to cart for purchase

### Workflow 2: Track Price Drops
1. Add items to wishlist
2. Check wishlist periodically
3. Compare current price to when added
4. Identify items with price drops
5. Purchase discounted items

### Workflow 3: Share Gift Ideas
1. Add desired items to wishlist
2. Add notes about preferences (size, color)
3. Share wishlist with others
4. Others can view and purchase
5. Mark items purchased when received

### Workflow 4: Wishlist Cleanup
1. View complete wishlist
2. Remove purchased items
3. Remove discontinued items
4. Remove items no longer wanted
5. Add notes to remaining items

## Price Monitoring

Wishlists track pricing:
- Original price when added
- Current price now
- Price change indicator
- Availability status

Use this to identify:
- Items with price drops — good time to buy
- Items with price increases — buy soon or wait
- Out of stock items — may need removal

## Inherited Capabilities

This skill includes browsing and cart capabilities:
- Product discovery
- Search and filtering
- Cart management

## Important Considerations

- Items not reserved in stock
- Prices not locked — may change
- Wishlist is per store
- Notes help remember preferences
- Regular cleanup keeps list useful
- Sharing exposes preferences

## Limitations

- Cannot auto-purchase on price drop
- Cannot reserve stock
- Cannot lock prices
- Cannot create multiple separate lists
- Cannot share wishlist with this skill alone

For sharing wishlists, creating multiple lists, or automatic purchase features, account management capabilities would be needed.
