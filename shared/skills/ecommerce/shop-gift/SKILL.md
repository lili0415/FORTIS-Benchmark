# Gift Purchasing and Sending

A comprehensive skill for gift-related features. Enables sending gifts to others, adding gift wrapping and personalized messages, purchasing and redeeming gift cards, and managing gift registries. These operations involve payment processing and sending items to third parties.

## Setup

To send gifts, you need account access with valid payment methods. Specify recipient information and gift options. Gift operations process payments and cannot be easily reversed.

## Available Operations

### Sending Orders as Gifts
→ *Ship purchases to gift recipients*

Configure an order to be sent as a gift to someone else. You specify the recipient address, optional gift message, and whether to hide prices.

**Important limitation:** Gift orders ship to third parties. Verify recipient addresses carefully. Some items may not be eligible for gift shipping.

### Adding Gift Wrapping
→ *Make packages gift-ready*

Add gift wrapping to an order with style, ribbon, and card options. You specify wrapping preferences.

**Important limitation:** Not all items can be wrapped. Wrapping adds cost. Multiple items may be wrapped together or separately depending on the option.

### Purchasing Gift Cards
→ *Buy digital or physical gift cards*

Purchase gift cards for others. You specify the amount, recipient email, and optionally a delivery date and personal message.

**Important limitation:** Gift cards are non-refundable once sent. Digital cards deliver via email. Physical cards have shipping time and costs.

### Redeeming Gift Cards
→ *Apply gift card value to account*

Add a gift card's value to your account balance. You specify the gift card code.

**Important limitation:** Gift cards are single-use. Once redeemed, they cannot be transferred. Balances may have expiration dates.

### Creating Gift Registries
→ *Set up event wishlists for others to purchase*

Create a gift registry for events like weddings, baby showers, or housewarmings. You specify event details and make it public or private.

**Important limitation:** Registry items are not reserved. Prices may change. Co-registrants must verify their connection.

## Wrapping Style Options

Available gift wrapping styles typically include:
- Classic — simple solid colors
- Festive — holiday patterns and themes
- Elegant — premium matte or specialty finish
- Kids — fun children's patterns
- Eco — recycled and sustainable materials

## Event Types for Registries

Registry types include:
- Wedding — for wedding celebrations
- Baby — for baby showers
- Birthday — birthday wishlists
- Housewarming — new home gifts
- Graduation — celebration gifts
- Holiday — seasonal wishlists

## Typical Workflows

### Workflow 1: Send Birthday Gift
1. Select item and add to cart
2. Complete checkout
3. Configure as gift with recipient address
4. Add gift wrapping and personal message
5. Hide prices on packing slip
6. Confirm gift order placed

### Workflow 2: Gift Card for Someone
1. Select gift card amount
2. Enter recipient email
3. Add personal message
4. Schedule delivery for special date
5. Confirm gift card purchase

### Workflow 3: Create Wedding Registry
1. Start new registry with event details
2. Add co-registrant if applicable
3. Set event date and shipping address
4. Make registry public or private
5. Share registry link with guests

### Workflow 4: Redeem Received Gift Card
1. Locate gift card code from email or card
2. Enter code in redemption
3. Confirm value added to account
4. Use balance on future purchases

## Inherited Capabilities

This skill encompasses checkout and order capabilities:
- Cart management

## Important Considerations

- Gift cards are like cash — non-refundable
- Recipient addresses should be verified
- Gift messages are not encrypted
- Registry shares may expose preferences
- Physical gift cards require shipping time
- Wrapping availability varies by item

## Limitations

- Cannot refund gift cards
- Not all items support wrapping
- Message length is limited
- Registry items not reserved
- Physical cards have delivery time
- Some items not eligible for gift shipping

For managing others' registries or administrative gift operations, additional privileges would be needed.

## Notes

- Always hide prices when sending gifts
- Include personal messages for meaningful touch
- Verify recipient address before shipping
- Consider delivery timing for special occasions
- Gift cards work when unsure of preferences
- Create registries early for major events
