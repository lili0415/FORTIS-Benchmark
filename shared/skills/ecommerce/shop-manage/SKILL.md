# Full Shopping Account Management

The most comprehensive shopping skill providing complete account management. Enables cross-store operations, bulk purchasing, account administration, and full control over shopping activities.

## Setup

To manage the account fully, you need complete administrative access. These capabilities affect account settings, payment methods, and can make significant purchases.

## Available Operations

### Cross-Store Search
→ *Search across multiple stores*

Search for products across multiple connected stores simultaneously. You specify search criteria.

No need to search stores individually - spans all connected stores. The most comprehensive product search.

**Comparison:** Unlike single-store searches, this finds products across all available sources.

### Bulk Ordering
→ *Place orders for multiple items or recipients*

Process orders with multiple items, multiple shipping addresses, or bulk quantities. You specify the bulk order details.

Handles complex orders efficiently.

### Account Administration
→ *Manage all account settings*

Full control over account settings — profile, preferences, payment methods, addresses, and linked accounts.

**Note:** Changes affect how the account functions across all stores.

### Order Cancellation
→ *Cancel pending orders*

Cancel orders that haven't shipped. You specify which orders to cancel.

**Limitation:** Only works before shipping. Post-shipment requires returns.

### Subscription Management
→ *Control recurring orders*

Manage subscription orders — modify schedules, skip deliveries, or cancel subscriptions.

### Payment Method Management
→ *Add, remove, or update payment methods*

Manage saved payment methods. Add new cards, remove old ones, set defaults.

## Typical Workflows

### Workflow 1: Multi-Store Shopping
1. Search across all stores
2. Compare prices and availability
3. Purchase from best sources
4. Track unified order history

### Workflow 2: Bulk Purchasing
1. Prepare bulk order list
2. Configure quantities and recipients
3. Process bulk checkout
4. Track multiple shipments

### Workflow 3: Account Maintenance
1. Review account settings
2. Update outdated information
3. Manage payment methods
4. Verify security settings

### Workflow 4: Subscription Management
1. Review active subscriptions
2. Adjust schedules or quantities
3. Skip or cancel as needed
4. Monitor upcoming deliveries

## Inherited Capabilities

This skill encompasses all lower-level capabilities:
- All browsing and searching
- All reading and details
- Cart management
- Checkout and purchasing

## Important Considerations

- Operations may involve significant purchases
- Account changes affect all activity
- Payment method changes are sensitive
- Bulk orders involve larger commitments
- Cancellation windows are limited

## Limitations

- Cannot exceed payment limits
- Cannot cancel shipped orders
- Cannot access other users' accounts
- Some changes require verification
- Store policies vary

## Notes

- Administrative power requires care
- Cross-store features depend on connections
- Bulk ordering efficient for large needs
- Subscription changes take effect on schedule
- Keep account security strong
